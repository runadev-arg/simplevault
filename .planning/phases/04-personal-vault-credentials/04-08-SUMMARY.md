## Key design decisions (operator-confirmed)

### `pointerdown` over `pointermove`

`pointermove` fires on every pixel of mouse drift — even involuntary
nudges from a stationary mouse (vibration, dirt on the optical sensor)
keep firing it. That would mask actual idleness and largely defeat the
point of an idle-lock. `pointerdown` only fires on a deliberate click /
tap — a clear "user is here" signal. `keydown` covers keyboard-only
flows.

### `visibilitychange` is ACTIVITY, not a TRIGGER

Two losing alternatives were considered:

1. **Lock immediately on `document.hidden`.** Would mean every Cmd-Tab
   wipes `master_DEK`, forcing the user to re-derive Argon2id (~600 ms
   on a laptop) every time they glance at Slack. Operator rejected: the
   threat model is "laptop walked away from for 15 min", not "user
   alt-tabbed for 3 seconds".
2. **Tighter deadline while hidden** (e.g. 5 min hidden vs 15 min
   visible). Adds state-machine complexity for a marginal threat-model
   delta; operator chose the simpler invariant — same 15-min idle
   regardless of focus.

Chosen behaviour: the `visibilitychange` event is a tracked activity
event that resets the timer. Tab return = activity. This is the
operator-confirmed default.

### No server notification on auto-lock

Auto-lock means "wipe `master_DEK` from RAM and bounce to `/login`",
NOT "remote logout". The user's `__Host-refresh` cookie + server-side
session remain valid until natural expiry. This is REQ-WEBSEC-007 and
Key Link 4 of the Phase 04 INDEX:

> The user is "locked" client-side only. Server session may still be
> valid — losing physical access to the laptop within the cookie
> lifetime is still a risk vector covered by the explicit-logout flow,
> the recovery-key revocation flow, and (Phase 05) the operator
> session-revocation tool.

### Operator runbook caveat

`Plan 04-12` will own the runbook extension. The required note:

> `auto_lock` is **NOT** a remote logout. If a user reports a stolen
> laptop, do NOT rely on auto-lock — issue a server-side session
> revocation (Phase 05 tooling) AND have them rotate their secret key.
